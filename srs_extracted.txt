1.1  Background and Necessity for the Application 
 
Employee onboarding and corporate training require organizations to 
communicate policies, procedures, role expectations, compliance requirements, 
workflows, and job-specific responsibilities accurately. In many organizations, 
this information is distributed across policy documents, Standard Operating 
Procedures (SOPs), role descriptions, FAQs, process manuals, and internal 
guidelines. 
 
Traditional onboarding often requires HR teams, managers, or trainers to 
manually review these documents and create training plans, learning modules, 
checklists, tasks, and assessments for different employees. This process can be 
time-consuming, inconsistent, difficult to personalize, and challenging to update 
whenever organizational documents change. 
 
The ‘SkillSprint AI’ application automatically analyzes company policies, role 
descriptions, process documents, SOPs, and FAQs to create personalized 
onboarding experiences. It generates role-specific learning plans, modules, 
checklists, tasks, quizzes, assessments, and progress recommendations while 
ensuring that generated content remains traceable to approved company 
documents. 
 
The application also verifies generated outputs using an independent Python-
based validation pipeline to identify missing requirements, unsupported 
statements, contradictions, duplicated learning items, incorrect role 
assignments, and hallucinations. 
 
The goal is to develop a reliable, secure, traceable, and intelligent Generative AI 
application that reduces manual onboarding effort while improving 
personalization, consistency, compliance coverage, and training quality.

1.2  Proposed Solution 
 
The proposed solution introduces a Generative AI-powered, Web-based 
SkillSprint AI application developed using Python and Generative AI APIs. 
The application allows administrators to upload organizational documents 
such as: 
• Company policies 
• HR policies 
• Role descriptions 
• Standard Operating Procedures 
• Process manuals 
• Compliance documents 
• Department guidelines 
• Frequently Asked Questions 
• Safety instructions 
• Employee handbooks 
The application validates and processes the uploaded PDF and DOCX 
documents and extracts their content into traceable sections or chunks. It 
stores information such as Document ID, Section ID, document version, 
effective date, and source reference.  
The application also creates a structured Role Requirement Matrix from the 
approved company documents. This matrix defines the mandatory and optional 
requirements applicable to each job role and serves as the ground-truth 
reference for validating the onboarding content generated by Generative AI. 
When an employee profile and job role are selected, the application identifies 
the relevant company documents and requirements. It then generates a 
personalized onboarding plan containing learning modules, learning objectives, 
checklists, practical tasks, quizzes, role-specific activities, milestones, 
assessments, and progress recommendations.

1.3  Purpose of the Document 
 
The purpose of this document is to outline the design, functionality, and 
implementation plan of the proposed SkillSprint AI application. This document 
ensures a shared understanding of the project objectives and provides a 
structured roadmap for the development, testing, and deployment of the 
application. It is intended for project stakeholders, developers, evaluators, HR 
professionals, training managers, administrators, and organizational learning 
teams. 
1.4  Scope of Project 
 
The scope of this project is to design and implement a Generative AI-powered, 
Web-based SkillSprint AI application. The application will analyze company 
policies, role descriptions, SOPs, process documents, compliance requirements, 
and FAQs to generate personalized onboarding plans, learning modules, 
checklists, quizzes, role-specific tasks, and progress assessments. 

--- PAGE 29 ---
 
 
© Aptech Limited 
The application will use Python and Generative AI APIs to generate structured 
onboarding content. An independent Python validation pipeline will verify 
coverage, source traceability, role relevance, contradictions, and unsupported 
content. It aims to reduce manual training preparation, improve personalization, 
maintain policy alignment, and provide consistent and traceable onboarding 
experiences. 
Direct integration with live HRMS, payroll, enterprise identity-management 
applications, or commercial Learning Management Application falls outside the 
mandatory scope of this project. 
 
 
1.5  Constraints 
 
The SkillSprint AI project faces certain constraints such as dependency on  
quality, completeness, consistency, and accuracy of company policies, role 
descriptions, SOPs, process documents, and FAQs. 
 
The effectiveness of generated onboarding content depends on the quality of 
source documents, prompt design, GenAI model behavior, context limitations, 
API availability, and the quality of the Python validation logic. Different 
Generative AI executions may produce different wording or organization even 
when the same documents and prompts are used. 
 
Differences may also occur between the requirements identified by the 
Generative AI pipeline and those validated through deterministic Python rules.

Creating a SkillSprint AI application involves several steps and components. The 
following are the functional requirements: 
i. User Authentication – The application should provide secure login for 
authorized users. 
 
ii. Role-Based Access Control – Access should differ for administrators, 
training managers, reviewers, managers, and employees. 
 
iii. Employee Profile Management – Employee role, department, 
experience, joining date, and relevant training information should be 
maintained. 
 
iv. Role Management – Job roles and associated onboarding requirements 
should be maintained. 
 
v. Document Upload – Authorized users should be able to upload company 
documents. 
 
vi. Document Validation – File type, duplicate status, metadata, and 
version should be validated. 
 
vii. Document Parsing – Python should extract usable content from 
uploaded documents. 
 
viii. Document Chunking – Documents should be divided into traceable 
chunks. 
 
ix. Source Metadata Management – Every chunk should maintain 
document and section references.

1.7  Non-Functional Requirements 
 
There are several non-functional requirements that should be fulfilled by the application 
which are as follows: 
1. Performance: The application should generate and validate a standard 
personalized onboarding plan within 30 seconds under normal API and 
network conditions. 
2. Scalable: The application should support at least 1,000 employee 
profiles, 100 job roles, and 1,000 organizational documents without 
requiring complete redesigning.

3. Usable: The application should provide an intuitive and user-friendly 
interface for employees, administrators, reviewers, HR teams, and training 
managers. 
 
4. Accuracy and Grounding: Mandatory onboarding plans should achieve 
100% coverage of the approved mandatory Role Requirement Matrix before 
final approval. Mandatory generated learning items should contain valid 
source references. 
 
5. Available: The application should remain available during competition 
evaluation with at least 99% uptime under normal conditions, excluding 
external GenAI API outages.

• Validation status

Evaluators may ask the application to generate training about a topic not 
contained in the approved company documents. 
The application should refuse, flag insufficient information, or route the request 
for manual review rather than invent company rules.
